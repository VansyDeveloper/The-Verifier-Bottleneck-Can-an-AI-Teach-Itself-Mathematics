What these runs show05 / 05

Evidence supports the hypothesis; final validation is still pending

What we observe

Mean performance rises with verifier quality.

Reliable and misleading regimes differ by 3.9 pp at the final step.

Realized TPR and FPR confirm that the verifiers behaved as intended.

Limits

Only three seeds. The 95% confidence interval for gain includes zero in every regime.

No regime plateaued across all three seeds; neither the 90% nor 95% threshold was reached.

The base-solved metric contains only 3-4 problems per seed, too few to assess forgetting.

The exploration pilot found Δεparse ≤ 0 and εcorrect = 0 on eight problems and one seed.

Stop new local training runs. If stronger evidence is needed, first reevaluate the nine saved adapters on a larger fixed set. Reserve the full H2 protocol for GPUs: 25 regimes × 3 seeds, at least 600 steps.
